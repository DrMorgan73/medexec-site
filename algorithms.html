Clinical

The medicine.

01 · CAUSATION VALIDATOR

Check the injury against the accident.

Flags claimed injuries that do not fit the accident mechanism — and passes the fragile patients who legitimately get hurt easier.

02 · TIMELINE COHERENCE AUDIT

Find the gaps in treatment.

Maps every dated event on the claim and flags gaps the other side will attack — with what they will argue, mapped to each gap.

07 · IME QUALITY SCORER

Grade every IME report.

Scores independent medical exams for thoroughness, bias signals, and defensibility before you rely on them.

17 · MALPRACTICE TRIAGE ENGINE

Screen malpractice in five steps.

Runs the four D's — duty, deviation, damages, direct cause — against guidelines, with a statute-of-limitations gate up front.

Fraud & Billing

The money trail.

03 · FRAUD PATTERN ENGINE

Score every provider.

Upcoding, unbundling, phantom billing, referral mills. Each provider gets a score from 0 to 100 — investigative leads for your team, never accusations.

08 · BILL REVIEW ENGINE

Catch inflated bills line by line.

Usual, customary, and reasonable checks. Duplicate charges. Services with no clinical note behind them. Every flag carries its line item and its reason.

Case Economics

The value of the claim.

04 · DEMAND PACKAGE AUDITOR

Audit the demand before it goes out.

Checks every demand package for completeness, inflated specials, and missing records — plus a Colossus value-driver check so you know your exposure before the carrier runs it.

05 · RESERVE SETTER

Set reserves with data, not gut.

Estimates claim reserve ranges from the injury, the jurisdiction, and the venue — so reserves reflect the case, not the quarter.

06 · SETTLEMENT PREDICTOR

Predict the settlement range.

Reads case features and historical patterns to forecast where the settlement lands — before the first offer goes out.

12 · SUBROGATION FINDER

Find money you did not know you had.

Reads the accident facts and the coverage picture to surface subrogation opportunities your team might have missed.

13 · COVERAGE MAPPER

Map the policy to the damages.

Lines up every claimed damage against the policy — and flags the gaps and exclusions before they surprise you.

14 · LIEN NEGOTIATOR

Know which liens will move.

Evaluates medical liens for negotiability and suggests reduction targets — so you negotiate from data, not hope.

15 · CASE VALUATION ENGINE

Value the whole case in one number.

Combines liability, damages, venue, and carrier behavior into a single defensible valuation with every input cited.

Litigation

The courtroom.

09 · DEPOSITION PREP GENERATOR

Walk in with the outline written.

Builds a deposition outline from the medical records and prior testimony — the questions the records demand, organized and cited.

10 · EXPERT WITNESS MATCHER

Match the expert to the case.

Matches your case needs to the right expert specialty — and runs the conflict check before you make the call.

11 · VENUE ANALYZER

Know the room before you enter it.

Reads jurisdiction and venue tendencies for your case type — verdict ranges, timelines, and what to expect.

16 · LITIGATION TIMELINE FORECASTER

See the road from filing to resolution.

Forecasts litigation milestones and costs from filing day to the end — so you can plan the case, not just react to it.
